1 · Warm-up: ¿qué palabra falta?

Click the word that completes each sentence. The page tells you why. Read each sentence aloud in Spanish.

2 · Round 1 · Tabú

One team, one phone. One person taps New card, looks at the word, and explains it in Spanish without saying the word or the three forbidden words. The others guess. If you don't know how to explain it, talk around it: Es algo que se usa para… / Es una persona que… / Es la etapa de la vida en que…

Two bonus points for a clue with alguien / algo que + verb. The guessers can ask: ¿Es una persona que tenga…? (unknown antecedent → subjunctive, Cap. 16)

—

Tap “New card”

Team score: 0  When time is up, the team with the most points wins. Use the 60-second timer for each turn.

3 · Round 2 · Busco a alguien que…

In pairs. Pick the same scene number but different roles, and do not show your card: tell your partner the information in Spanish. A is looking for someone and doesn't know who exists: ask ¿Conoces a alguien que…? B knows two real people and describes them: Conozco a una persona que… At the end, decide together: whom should A meet, and under what conditions?

Watch the contrast. A, who doesn't know: busco a alguien que sea… B, who knows: conozco a alguien que es… Unknown → subjunctive; known → indicative.

To decide, use at least two: con tal de que… · a menos que… · en caso de que… · para que… · sin que…

4 · Round 3 · Una vida en cuatro frases

Teams of four. The team gets a character in the middle of life and three vocabulary words. Each person adds one sentence in Spanish, one for each part of the life:

  1. La infancia: how life was. Cuando era niño/a, … (imperfect)
  2. La juventud: what happened. A los veinte años, … (preterite)
  3. La madurez: what the character looks for. Ahora busca a alguien que… (subjunctive)
  4. La vejez: what the character is going to do, and on what condition. Va a… con tal de que…

Each target word used meaningfully earns one point.

Tap “New character.”

Points: 0  One bonus point if all four stages were covered.

5 · Homework · One recording

One recording, no reading. You will get the link in class. The directions are in English; you record in Spanish.

Busco a alguien que… (60–90 seconds)

Record a 60–90 second message in Spanish. Do not read: talk to the camera as if you were telling a classmate.

You are speaking as a character in his or her middle years (madurez), invented or approved in advance. Tell us:

1. Who is the character? Name, age, and one detail about his or her life now.
2. What does the character look for and not find? Two sentences like: Busca a alguien que… / No hay nadie que… / Necesita un amigo que…
3. What is the character going to do in old age (la vejez)? Use ir a + infinitive, and attach two conditions or purposes: con tal de que… / a menos que… / para que… / en caso de que…

Use at least four words about relationships or stages of life (la pareja, el noviazgo, llevarse bien, romper con, la juventud, la madurez, la vejez…).

Before you submit, ask yourself:

  • Can a classmate understand me?
  • Did I say two things my character looks for and cannot find, with que + a verb?
  • Did I use va a + infinitive, and at least two conditions or purposes?
  • Did I keep talking with details, instead of stopping after one sentence?
